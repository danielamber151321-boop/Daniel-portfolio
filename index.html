ABOUT

Hi, I'm Daniel.

I'm a video creator and video editor focused on helping brands and businesses turn ideas into compelling visual content.

I take concepts from idea to execution — from developing storyboards and visual directions to editing and producing the final video. My goal is to create content that not only looks good, but is tailored to the intended audience and communicates the message effectively.

I've spent the past three years working in video production, including freelance work with clients through Fiverr and collaborations with creative teams and studios. I've worked with clients and organizations including Atext Studio, Vatex Lab, and Megzapixel, among others, and I currently maintain a partnership with Atext Studio.

My strongest areas are video editing, AI-powered video creation, AI image generation, 2D animation, and visual storytelling. I'm comfortable working with timelines, cuts, transitions, effects, color grading, sound design, and the details that turn raw footage or an idea into a polished final piece.

My approach is simple: understand the idea, understand the audience, and turn it into something worth watching.
